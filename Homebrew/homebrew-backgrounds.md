# HOMEBREW BACKGROUNDS

The sixteen backgrounds in the 2024 Player's Handbook each cover a unique combination of three ability scores, but only 16 of the 20 possible combinations. These four homebrew backgrounds complete the set:

| Background | Abilities | Skills | Tool | Feat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gambler | DEX, INT, CHA | Deception, Insight | Gaming Set (choice) | Lucky |
| Gladiator | STR, CON, CHA | Athletics, Performance | Smith's Tools | Savage Attacker |
| Miner | STR, CON, INT | Athletics, Nature | Mason's Tools | Tough |
| Missionary | STR, WIS, CHA | Persuasion, Survival | Cook's Utensils | Healer |

---

### GAMBLER

Ability Scores: Dexterity, Intelligence, Charisma

Feat: Lucky (see chapter 5)

Skill Proficiencies: Deception and Insight

Tool Proficiency: Choose one kind of Gaming Set (see chapter 6)

Equipment: Choose A or B: (A) Gaming Set (same as above), Dagger, Fine Clothes, 2 Pouches, 31 GP; or (B) 50 GP

You made your living at the gaming table, where quick fingers, a quicker mind, and an honest-looking face were worth more than any trade. You learned to count cards and calculate odds without moving your lips, to read an opponent's tell across a smoky room, and to smile warmly while holding a hand worth a season's wages. Some nights you left rich; other nights you left through the window. Either way, you learned that fortune favors those who make their own luck.

---

### GLADIATOR

Ability Scores: Strength, Constitution, Charisma

Feat: Savage Attacker (see chapter 5)

Skill Proficiencies: Athletics and Performance

Tool Proficiency: Smith's Tools

Equipment: Choose A or B: (A) Spear, Smith's Tools, Costume, Healer's Kit, Traveler's Clothes, 15 GP; or (B) 50 GP

You fought for the roar of the crowd. Whether in a grand coliseum or a fighting pit behind a dockside tavern, you learned that surviving a bout was only half the job—the other half was making the audience love you for it. Between matches you repaired your own arms and armor, tended your wounds, and studied your rivals. You carry the scars of your victories and the showmanship that earned you your freedom, your fame, or both.

---

### MINER

Ability Scores: Strength, Constitution, Intelligence

Feat: Tough (see chapter 5)

Skill Proficiencies: Athletics and Nature

Tool Proficiency: Mason's Tools

Equipment: Choose A or B: (A) Sledgehammer, Mason's Tools, Hooded Lantern, Oil (3 flasks), Rope, Traveler's Clothes, 30 GP; or (B) 50 GP

You spent your years underground, swinging a pick by lantern light and hauling ore up switchback tunnels. The work hardened your body, and the dangers sharpened your mind: you learned to read the stone itself—which seams promised silver, which timbers were rotting, and which silences meant the air had gone bad. Miners who didn't pay attention didn't come home, and you always came home.

---

### MISSIONARY

Ability Scores: Strength, Wisdom, Charisma

Feat: Healer (see chapter 5)

Skill Proficiencies: Persuasion and Survival

Tool Proficiency: Cook's Utensils

Equipment: Choose A or B: (A) Quarterstaff, Cook's Utensils, Holy Symbol, Bedroll, Book (prayers), Traveler's Clothes, 16 GP; or (B) 50 GP

You carried your faith beyond the temple walls, over mountain passes and through lands where your god's name had never been spoken. You hauled your own supplies, cooked for strangers to open their doors, and bound the wounds of anyone who needed it, believer or not. You learned that a full belly and a healed hand win more converts than any sermon—though you gave the sermons too.
